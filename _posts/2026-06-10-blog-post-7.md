As part of my work and tasks as a researcher, I have spent some time reviewing recent publications in the field related to my thesis. This allows me to keep up with the latest developments and progress in this case related to biomass estimation in pastures. 
A couple of things to highlight is that UAV-based pasture biomass estimation has achieved substantial technical maturity, the irruption of high-resolution satellite imagery and recent reviews available. However, a critical bottleneck persists: model transferability across space and time.
