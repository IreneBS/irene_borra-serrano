Active project

Digital tools for sustainable agriculture · ICA-CSIC · 2025–2028

ECODigital proposes the application of technologies in the fields of remote sensing, sensorisation and artificial intelligence for the development and validation of digital tools for agriculture. Digital technology will be used through ground and remote sensing to monitor crops and their habitat. The entire digitisation workflow will be covered mainly through low-cost hardware and open source software. ECODigital will be implemented in plots of the experimental farm La Poveda (managed by ICA-CSIC), through three pilot scenarios covering a wide range of agroecosystems: maize, vineyard and floral margins. In addition to promoting the use of technologies in the agricultural sector, this proposal aims to enhance the resources of the La Poveda estate and ICA as reference center for R&D in the digitisation of agriculture.

Objectives
O.1.

Pilot Scenario 1

Precise and selective weed control using remote sensing and machine/deep learning to reduce the use of pesticides

O.2

Pilot Scenario 2

Digital Characterization of Functional Traits in Vineyards Using Remote and Ground-Based Sensors for Agroecological Management

O.3

Pilot Scenario 3

Monitoring floral resources using remote sensing, ground-based sensors, and ML/DL to preserve (plants and pollinators) biodiversity in managed weed communities
